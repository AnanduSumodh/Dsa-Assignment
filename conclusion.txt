FINAL CONCLUSION

For the given student scores, both Max Heap and Linear Search find the
highest score as 95.

The Max Heap keeps the highest score at the root, so the maximum can be
accessed in O(1) time. Inserting a new score takes O(log n) in the worst
case because the new value may move upward through the heap.

Linear Search finds the maximum by checking the scores one by one, so
finding the maximum takes O(n) time.

Therefore, for continuously maintaining and retrieving the highest
student score as new scores are inserted, the Max Heap is more suitable.
